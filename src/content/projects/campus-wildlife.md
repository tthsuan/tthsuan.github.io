My MSc research examined bird diversity and environmental relationships on the Tsinghua University campus. The related bird paper was based entirely on this thesis.

A later campus biodiversity project, supported by SEE Foundation and He Yi Green Foundation, used camera traps and acoustic recorders for wildlife monitoring and public outreach.
